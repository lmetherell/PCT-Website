Our Pacific Crest Trail Journey

The pictures represent moments from our journey along the Pacific Crest Trail. We started our journey on May 3, 1996 at the US/Mexican border and started hiking north through Southern California. The desert is beautiful. We stopped along many small towns along the way, including Agua Dulce, CA where we picked up a resupply box at the post office. One of the pictures is of the content of our box. We continued on and eventually made our way up the top of Forester Pass. The sign at the top noted it is 13,200 feet (4,023 m) in elevation. There was snow so the ice axe and crampons came in handy. One of the crown jewels on the Pacific Crest Trail is the 210 mile John Muir Trail starting at Mount Whitney and ending at Toulumne Meadows in Yosemite. I had to end my hike early due to feet issues but my husband continued on until September of 2026 and ended his hike at White Pass, WA. He had approximately 357 miles left of the approximately 2,650 mile trail to complete the hike but had to end it early due to an illness. Although neither of us accomplished our goal of completing the entire trail as a through hike we had an amazing experience. We were able to experience the beauty and challenges of the trail, meet amazing people, and learn a lot along the way. It is an experience we will always remember.
