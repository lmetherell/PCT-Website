Our Journey on the Pacific Crest Trail

After two years of marriage, my husband and I decided to attempt a through hike the Pacific Crest Trail in 1996. The trail crosses three states, California, Oregon, and Washington, starting on the Mexican/US border and ending in Canada. There are large elevation changes, from 110 feet (34 m) to 13,153 feet (4,009 m). It passes through several national parks, including Sequoia, Yosemite, Crater Lake and others. Due to the nature of the hike and diverse landscapes, including deserts to snow filled passes, there was much to prepare. For more information on the trail go to: Pacific Crest Trail Association This is a brief glimpse into our journey.
