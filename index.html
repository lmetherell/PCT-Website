Our Journey on the Pacific Crest Trail

My husband and I decided to attempt a through hike of the Pacific Crest Trail in 1996. The trail crosses three states, California, Oregon, and Washington, starting on the Mexican/US border and ending in Canada. The trail has been designated by Congress as a National Scenic Trial. It is approximately 2,650 miles (4,265 km) long. There are large elevation changes, from 110 feet (34 m) to 13,153 feet (4,009 m). It passes through several national parks, including Sequoia, Yosemite, Crater Lake and others. Due to the nature of the hike and variety of terrain and climates, including deserts to snow filled passes, there was much to prepare. We were fortunate to have passed through many wonderful towns and met a lot of great people on the journey. For more information on the trail go to: Pacific Crest Trail Association This is a brief glimpse into our journey.
