Preparing for a Through Hike of the Pacific Crest Trail in 1996

My husband and I spent approximately one year preparing for our through hike physically and logistically. Physically we would hike with weighted backpacks as often as possible. We also took a winter survival course up in the Mount Lassen area which included the use of an ice axe and crampons. Logistically we conducted a lot of research and planning on completing a through hike of the Pacific Crest Trail. In addition to the physical training below is a list of some of the steps we took to prepare for the hike:

  • We researched the trail and the best time to hike it. We decided to start in early May and end in late September.
  • We created a resupply plan which included mailing ourselves boxes of supplies to post offices along the trail.
  • When purchasing our equipment and supplies, including backpacks, tents, sleeping bags, and other essentials, we made finding the lightest weight equipement and supplies a high priority. We even learned to sew so would could make light weight clothes and other items. We also modified purchased items to make them lighter.
  • We took a wilderness first aid and survival skills course.
  • We obtained all necessary permits.
  • We prepared our affairs at home to be away for an extended period.
  • We researched the terrain and climate along the trail to ensure we had all the appropriate equipment and supplies to meet the demands of the trail
